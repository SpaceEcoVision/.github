# Space EcoVision

*Every road to space starts on Earth.*

**Vision:** An Earth cared for well enough to carry people into space, and space kept open enough to become a second home for generations to come.

**Mission:** Space EcoVision studies how space infrastructure is built and how it shapes economies and the natural world. Its aim is to help people today care for Earth's resources, and to learn what policy and access future generations will need to live and work in space and on Earth. Those choices are being made now. The research cannot wait.
