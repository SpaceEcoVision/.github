# Space EcoVision

**Measuring the space economy — and what it does to the ground.**

We are an independent research group using satellite data and open economic models
to measure the growth of the space economy on the ground — spaceports, manufacturing,
and the infrastructure of launch — and to build the case that expanding into space
lets Earth recover. We help the industry scale quickly and responsibly. Earth and
space, treated as one economy.

### What we're building

- **Earth observation** — mapping the growth of the space economy from Sentinel-2
  imagery (we tracked Starbase's buildout, 2019–2023), moving from statistical
  indices to computer vision. Default targets are US spaceports.
- **[Space economy indicators](https://github.com/SpaceEcoVision/space-economy-indicators)** — ecological-economics models of how resources move between Earth and orbit.
- **[Space policy corpus](https://github.com/SpaceEcoVision/space-policy-corpus)** — a structured, searchable base of space treaties, policy, and launch records.

### Open by default

Our methods, code, and data are public as they develop — early, honest, and improving.
We are looking for collaborators: ecologists, machine-learning engineers, and
geospatial analysts who want this measured properly.
